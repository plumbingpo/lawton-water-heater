Keeping Your Geronimo Water Heater Running Longer

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, lift the T&P valve lever annually to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which reduces the risk of scalding and cuts down on scale. Tankless owners should plan on a descaling flush once a year in hard-water areas.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters run 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models usually run 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing warning signs such as discolored hot water, popping and rumbling, dampness under the tank, or showers that go cold sooner, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one with water on the floor.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Take a spent anode rod: replacing it is a quick, inexpensive repair, but once it's gone, the water begins corroding the tank itself, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a flooded utility room.

Typical Water Heater Issues in Geronimo

Whatever type of water heater your Geronimo home has, it tends to fail in a predictable way. Gas tank heaters tend to have trouble with pilot assemblies and thermocouples, sooty burners, and venting that doesn't draft. Electric tanks burn out elements and thermostats, particularly when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless heaters throw error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition faults, and flow sensor trouble, and many of those trace back to skipped descaling. Heat pump water heaters depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. Our technicians work on all four every week and stock the common parts to finish most repairs in one trip.
