Food Service Hot Water

Hot Water for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Dish machines, sanitizing sinks, and handwashing stations all compete for the same hot water, and a heater that's undersized or failing shows up first as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and stacks of dirty plates. A high-temp dish machine usually needs final rinse water near 180°F, which almost always calls for a booster heater working alongside the main supply. Our food-service work covers high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and peak-demand sizing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Temperature is where many restaurant hot water problems start. When the main heater can't keep up, handwashing sinks run lukewarm, the three-compartment sink falls below temperature, and the dish machine struggles to sanitize during the rush. Our technicians verify temperatures from the tank all the way to the fixtures and document every visit in written reports your inspector and landlord can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and staging temporary hot water where possible.

Safety

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect flue draft and vent connections, confirm adequate combustion air, check relief valves and their discharge lines, verify expansion tanks are charged to the right pressure, and set mixing valves so water can be stored hot enough to control bacteria while reaching the tap at a safe temperature. We pull permits and schedule inspections where required. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. We keep a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test dates, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms, gets a more frequent service schedule.

After-Hours Service

Fast Commercial Water Heater Service

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Lawton Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties throughout Lawton and surrounding areas, and we schedule non-urgent work after hours or before opening so your doors stay open.

Preventive Care

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

Stopping failures before they happen is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters. Every Lawton Water Heater program is tailored to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and covers scheduled inspections, flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and verification of delivered water temperature.
